
# GhostCall

### Talk without exchanging phone numbers.

GhostCall is a browser-based temporary voice calling platform that lets people connect through simple, shareable links.

No accounts. No phone number exchange. No app installation.

🌐 **[Visit GhostCall](https://www.ghostcall.space/)**

---

## About

Not every conversation needs to become a permanent connection.

GhostCall makes it easy to start a voice conversation without sharing personal contact details. Create a call link, share it with someone, and talk directly from your browser.

## Features

- **1-to-1 Voice Calls** — Start temporary voice conversations.
- **Group Calls** — Bring up to four participants into one call.
- **Shareable Links** — Invite people using a simple call link.
- **No Signup Required** — Create or join calls without an account.
- **Browser-Based** — Works on supported mobile and desktop browsers.
- **Temporary Rooms** — Rooms are designed for temporary conversations rather than permanent contact connections.

## Use Cases

- Online buying and selling
- Meeting people online
- Quick work conversations
- Temporary group discussions

## Our Vision

We believe people should have more choice over how they communicate.

GhostCall is being built around a simple idea: a conversation shouldn't require exchanging permanent contact details.
